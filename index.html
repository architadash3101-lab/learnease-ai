🌍 Responsible AI & SDG 4 Impact

RESPONSIBLE AI

🔒 Privacy by design

Everything runs in the browser — no backend, no accounts, no API keys. The demo dataset is synthetic: no real student data is collected or stored. In a production deployment, LearnEase would follow data-minimisation (behavioural features only, no names shared with the model), on-device or school-hosted inference, and explicit parent consent under India’s DPDP Act and similar rules.

⚖️ Fairness check across groups

The fairness check compares model recall and average predicted risk across learner groups (Typical, Dyslexia, Dyscalculia, ADHD). A group is flagged if its false-negative rate differs from the overall rate by more than 10 percentage points.

🧑‍⚕️ Human-in-the-loop

LearnEase is an assist, not a decision-maker. Every alert explicitly asks the teacher to pair the model’s signal with their own judgement. Teachers can override any status, and no consequential action (extra support, parent contact, assessment changes) is taken without a human in the loop. Students always see why each question was chosen, and every prediction comes with a plain-language explanation.

⚠️ Limitations

  • All data here is synthetic and illustrative — real accuracy will differ.
  • The mock LLM is a dictionary + rule simplifier, not a true language model.
  • Translation uses a small demo glossary, not a production translation engine.
  • Risk scores are screening signals; they must never be used to label or rank children.
  • Read-aloud quality depends on the device’s speech engine.

💼 Business View

SUSTAINABILITY MODEL

🏫 Schools (main customer)

Inclusive schools and mainstream schools with SEN units buy per-student annual licences. Value: early learning-gap detection, less manual differentiation work, ready-made SEN-friendly lesson formats.

Revenue: school licence — ₹250–400 / student / year (estimate), billed per class or campus, with an inclusive-education onboarding package for teachers.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parents

Freemium: simplified lessons and 1 adaptive quiz per day free. Premium unlocks unlimited practice, full progress forecasting, parent alerts and the reminder agent.

Revenue: parent freemium plan — ₹199 / month (estimate) premium tier, converting engaged free users.

🤝 NGOs & CSR programmes

Education NGOs and corporate CSR programmes sponsor LearnEase for government and low-cost private schools, aligned with SDG 4 reporting needs.

Revenue: NGO & CSR partnerships — per-school sponsorship grants (estimate), with impact dashboards as the deliverable.

🧭 Why this works

Schools already spend teacher hours on manual differentiation; LearnEase automates the lesson-adaptation and early-warning parts, so one inclusive-education coordinator can support more students. The freemium parent plan funds engagement at home, and CSR sponsorship makes the school licence affordable in low-income contexts — a cross-subsidy model that keeps SDG 4 impact and revenue aligned.
